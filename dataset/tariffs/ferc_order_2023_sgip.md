# FERC Order No. 2023 & Small Generator Interconnection Procedures (SGIP)
## Improvements to Generator Interconnection Procedures and Agreements

### Section 2: Fast Track Process Thresholds
Interconnection customers proposing a generating facility of 5 MW or less may qualify for the standardized Fast Track Process under the Pro Forma SGIP if the facility meets technical initial screening criteria.

### Section 3: Cluster Study Transition & Withdrawal Penalties
1. Interconnection queues are processed in first-ready, first-served clusters.
2. Interconnection applicants failing initial screening or supplemental screening are assessed study costs for detailed system studies.
3. Uncured technical deficiencies submitted during formal review cycles lead to application termination to preserve queue velocity.
